What needs a measurement?

The glass body is 105.33 × 62.37 mm. Its visible area is 92.8 × 55.68 mm. The model uses the drawing’s offset.

Confirm header plugs, wire bends, the ribbon route, antenna size, and battery connector before CAD.

The 30 mm depth includes both walls. It does not prove the assembled hardware fits.

Access from the rear

Remove the device from the fridge. Insert a small plastic tool through each release slot. Press the clip toward the centre and lift the cover edge. Two slots sit on top; two sit underneath. Open the cover to reach USB-C, RESET, and BOOT. The carrier holds the panel. The electronics have no printed mounts.

Clip sizes and magnet strength need checks for the chosen print material. The printed parts in this view use the exported CAD geometry. Validate the first print before fitting the panel.
